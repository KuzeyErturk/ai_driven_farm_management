# Papers Behind Paywall — Download via University Library

Access these through your university library portal (most universities have subscriptions to these journals). Search by DOI on Google Scholar and click "Access through your institution."

## 02 - Ceglar et al. (2019) — Agro-Climate Zones Migrating North
- **Journal:** Earth's Future (AGU/Wiley)
- **DOI:** 10.1029/2019EF001178
- **URL:** https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2019EF001178
- **Why important:** Scientific basis for why cross-country pooling is justified
